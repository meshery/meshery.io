Models that know how your infrastructure fits together.

Meshery models encode expert knowledge of how components relate and how a change to one affects the rest: what mounts what, what selects what, who may talk to whom. Meshery's policy engine evaluates every design as it is created, updated, or imported, automatically creating relationships and updating linked configuration when they form or dissolve. Think of it as the senior engineer's mental model, externalized, versioned, and enforceable.
