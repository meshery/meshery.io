# Vision

Meshery.io is the digital front door to Meshery, the place to understand what the project is, explore what it manages, and find a way in.

## Purpose

Meshery.io exists so that anyone evaluating, adopting, or contributing to Meshery can understand the project without reading its source code.

For most people, it is the first thing they encounter, and often the only thing they see before deciding whether to go further. The site succeeds when a visitor leaves knowing what Meshery does and where to go next.

## Goals

- Explain what Meshery is and who it is for, in terms a newcomer can understand and act on.
- Present the breadth of the ecosystem, its models, integrations, and catalog, as something a visitor can browse and search.
- Offer contributors a visible entry point into the community, its programs, and its events.
- Direct every reader onward to the right destination, whether that is the documentation, the application, or the community.
- Reflect the current state of the ecosystem without depending on manual upkeep.

## Principles

### Public web presence

Meshery.io is a website, and it is judged as one. A change is good if a visitor understands Meshery better, finds what they came for faster, or leaves with a clearer next step.

### Present, don't own

Most of what this site displays belongs to other projects. Models, integrations, catalog entries, and community activity originate elsewhere and are published here. When that data is wrong, it is corrected at its source rather than on the page that displays it.

What Meshery.io does own is the presentation: information architecture, layout, navigation, and the reading experience. That distinction decides where a fix belongs.

### Generated content is generated

Content produced by automation remains the responsibility of the system that generates it. Contributors improve those systems rather than editing their output by hand.

This keeps the site honest about the ecosystem it reflects, and it keeps a well-intentioned edit from being silently undone later.

### Documentation belongs to docs.meshery.io

This site says what exists and why it matters. Instructions, reference material, and task guidance belong to the documentation.

Where the two meet, the documentation is canonical and Meshery.io links to it. Duplicating it here creates a second copy that quietly goes stale.

### Collections define published content

Every kind of content published here is a declared collection with a defined shape and a predictable URL. New content types are introduced as deliberate additions to the site's information architecture, not as one-off pages.

## Boundaries and Non-Goals

Meshery.io is not the documentation site.

Meshery.io is not the Meshery application, and it does not manage infrastructure or talk to a Meshery Server.

Meshery.io is not the source of truth for the model registry or the catalog.

Meshery.io is not where project governance or maintainership is defined.

## Success Criteria

The vision is being met when:

- A newcomer can say what Meshery does after one visit.
- Someone looking for a specific integration finds it and reaches its documentation in a few clicks.
- A prospective contributor finds a way in without having to ask where to start.
- Content stays current because it is generated, not because someone remembered to update it.
- A contributor can tell, from this document alone, whether a proposed change belongs here or somewhere else.
